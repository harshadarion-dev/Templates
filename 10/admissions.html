Admissions

Join a diverse community of passionate learners and future leaders.

How to Apply

We look for highly motivated students ready to challenge themselves.

Step 1

Submit Application online

Fill out the general admissions application form and choose your desired major along with secondary interests.

Step 2

Provide Transcripts

Official high school or prior university transcripts must be submitted physically or verified electronically.

Important Dates & Deadlines

Stay on track with your application schedule.

Early Decision

November 1

Submit all materials for early consideration and priority housing options.

Regular Decision

January 15

Standard deadline for all incoming freshmen applications across all faculties.

Financial Aid

February 1

All FAFSA and scholarship materials due for final university grants processing.
