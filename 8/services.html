WHAT WE DO

Our Legal Practices

We offer comprehensive legal solutions across an array of disciplines, bringing focus, precision, and relentless advocacy to every case.

Criminal Defense

Facing criminal charges can fundamentally disrupt your life. We approach every case with immediate intervention, rigorous factual investigation, and robust courtroom defense. Our priority is protecting your constitutional rights and mitigating long-term damages to your freedom and reputation.

We handle everything from misdemeanors and DUIs to highly complex federal indictments and white-collar defense.

Family Law

Divorce and custody disputes require a delicate balance of aggressive advocacy and profound emotional intelligence. We mediate where possible and litigate fiercely when necessary.

Our team assists in asset segregation, spousal support, child custody, domestic violence restraining orders, and prenuptial agreements.

Corporate Law

Protecting a business's structural and financial integrity is critical to its longevity. We represent startups, mid-market enterprises, and massive public corporations across all operational litigations.

We draft intricate employment contracts, execute mergers and acquisitions, resolve shareholder disputes, and guarantee total regulatory compliance.

Real Estate

Commercial and residential real estate transactions demand an eagle eye for detail. We step in to secure your land titles, mitigate zoning disputes, and draft iron-clad lease formulations.

In addition, our litigators defend against foreclosure actions and handle contentious landlord-tenant relationships efficiently.
