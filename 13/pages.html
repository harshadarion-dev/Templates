Shipping & Delivery

How long does delivery take?

Standard shipping typically takes 5-7 business days. For custom-made or backordered items, please allow 3-4 weeks. You will receive a tracking link via email once your order has dispatched.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently, we ship within the contiguous United States, Canada, and select countries in Europe. International shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on weight and destination.

Returns & Exchanges

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be in original condition and packaging. Please note that a 10% restocking fee applies to large furniture items, and return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer.

What if my item arrives damaged?

If your furniture arrives damaged, please contact our support team within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the damage and the packaging. We will arrange for a replacement or professional repair at no cost to you.

Product Care

How should I clean my wooden furniture?

Wipe clean with a soft, dry cloth. Do not use harsh chemicals or abrasive cleaners, as they can damage the natural lacquer. For deeper cleaning, use a slightly damp cloth and wipe dry immediately.
