Common Questions

Frequently Asked

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend booking 12–18 months in advance for full planning, especially for peak season dates (March–June and September–November) in Savannah.

Can we customize this package?

Absolutely. Every wedding is unique, and we tailor our services to fit your specific needs, guest count, and event complexity.

Do you travel outside of Savannah?

Yes! While we specialize in Savannah and the Lowcountry, we love destination weddings throughout Georgia and the Southeast.

What is your payment structure?

We require a 50% retainer to secure your date, with the remaining balance due two weeks prior to your wedding day.
