Service Details

Comprehensive planning for your perfect celebration.

Overview

Full-Service Planning

Our comprehensive wedding planning service covers every aspect of your celebration from initial concept to final execution. We work closely with you to understand your vision, manage all vendors, coordinate timelines, and ensure every detail is perfect.

With over 12 years of experience and 500+ weddings planned, our team brings expertise, creativity, and meticulous attention to detail to every event we touch.

Process

How It Works

Initial Consultation

We begin with a complimentary consultation to understand your vision, style preferences, and budget. This is where the magic starts.

Design & Planning

Our team creates a custom design board, sources vendors, and develops a detailed timeline tailored to your unique celebration.

Vendor Coordination

We manage all vendor communications, contracts, and logistics, ensuring everyone is aligned with your vision.

Final Details & Rehearsal

We finalize all details, conduct the rehearsal, and prepare every element for your big day.

Your Perfect Day

On the day of your event, we handle everything behind the scenes so you can be fully present and enjoy every moment.
