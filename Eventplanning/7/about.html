Who We Are

Passionate About Creating Perfect Moments

Founded in 2014, Elle Wedding Planning began with a simple mission: to transform the stress of wedding planning into a joyful, memorable journey. What started as a small boutique service in Chicago has grown into one of the Midwest's most sought-after event planning studios.

We believe every love story deserves a celebration as unique as the couple themselves. Our approach combines meticulous attention to detail with creative vision, ensuring that every event we plan is not just beautiful, but deeply personal.
