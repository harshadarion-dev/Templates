The Story of

Marry Me Wedding Agency was founded in 2015 out of a passion for creating truly unique events. Over the years, we have organized more than 300 weddings of various scales — from intimate ceremonies to grand banquets for 500 guests.

Our philosophy is simple: every wedding should reflect the love story of two people. We do not use templates — each event is created individually, taking into account the character, tastes, and wishes of the couple.
