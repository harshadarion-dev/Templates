With over a decade of experience in luxury event planning, I have dedicated my career to transforming visions into breathtaking realities. Every celebration tells a story, and I am honored to be the storyteller behind your most precious moments.

Based in New York and available for destination events worldwide, I work with a curated network of the finest vendors, venues, and artisans to deliver experiences that exceed expectations.
