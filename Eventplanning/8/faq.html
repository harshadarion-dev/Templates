Common Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our services and process.

How far in advance should I book your services? +
We recommend booking at least 6-12 months in advance for weddings and large events. For smaller celebrations, 3-6 months is typically sufficient. However, we occasionally accommodate last-minute requests depending on availability.
What is included in the initial consultation? +
Our complimentary initial consultation includes a 60-minute meeting (in-person or virtual) where we discuss your vision, budget, timeline, and preferences. We'll provide initial recommendations and outline how we can bring your dream event to life.
Do you work with specific vendors or can I choose my own? +
We have a curated network of trusted vendors that we highly recommend, but you are absolutely welcome to bring your own vendors. We're happy to work with any professional vendor that aligns with your vision and budget.
What is your cancellation policy? +
Our cancellation policy is outlined in our service contract. Generally, deposits are non-refundable but may be transferable to a future date depending on timing. We understand that circumstances change and work with our clients to find the best solution.
Do you plan destination events? +
Yes! We love destination events and have planned celebrations across the United States and internationally. Travel fees apply and are discussed during the consultation phase.
Can you work within my budget? +
Absolutely. We believe every celebration deserves expert planning, regardless of budget size. During our consultation, we'll discuss your budget and create a plan that maximizes value while delivering an unforgettable experience.
What happens on the day of the event? +
On event day, our team arrives early to oversee setup, manages the timeline, coordinates with all vendors, handles any issues that arise, and ensures everything runs smoothly. You can relax and enjoy your celebration while we handle the details.
Do you offer payment plans? +
Yes, we offer flexible payment plans. Typically, we require a 50% deposit to secure your date, with the remaining balance due two weeks before the event. Custom payment schedules can be arranged for larger events.
