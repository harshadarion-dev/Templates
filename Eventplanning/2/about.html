About Alice Lu Events

Founded in 2012, Alice Lu Events has grown from a passionate dream into one of the most sought-after luxury event planning firms. Our journey began with a simple belief: that every celebration deserves to be extraordinary.

With over a decade of experience, we have curated unforgettable experiences for discerning clients across the globe. From intimate gatherings to grand galas, our team brings creativity, precision, and an unwavering commitment to excellence to every event we touch.

We believe that luxury is not just about opulence—it's about intention. Every detail, every moment, every interaction is thoughtfully designed to create a seamless experience that feels both effortless and extraordinary.
