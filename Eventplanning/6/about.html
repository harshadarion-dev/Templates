Our Story

Creating Magical Moments Since 2015

Belle Bodas & Events was founded with a simple yet powerful vision: to transform life's most precious moments into extraordinary celebrations. What began as a passion project in Park City, Utah has blossomed into a premier luxury event planning company serving clients across two stunning locations.

Our team of dedicated planners brings together decades of combined experience in hospitality, design, and logistics. We believe that every event tells a story, and we're here to help you write yours with elegance, creativity, and flawless execution.

From intimate gatherings to grand galas, we approach each event with fresh eyes and unwavering commitment to excellence. Our dual-location presence allows us to offer unique destination experiences in both the majestic mountains of Utah and the enchanting colonial streets of San Miguel de Allende.

Our Values

What We Stand For

Excellence in Every Detail

We believe that true luxury lies in the details. From the placement of each flower to the timing of every toast, we obsess over the elements that transform a good event into an extraordinary one.

Authentic Connections

Every celebration begins with understanding. We take the time to know our clients deeply—their stories, their dreams, their quirks—so we can create events that feel genuinely personal.

Creative Innovation

We constantly push boundaries, seeking fresh inspiration from art, fashion, travel, and culture. No two events we plan are ever the same, because no two love stories are identical.

Seamless Execution

Behind every effortless-looking event is meticulous planning. Our team's expertise in logistics ensures that your celebration unfolds smoothly, allowing you to be fully present in every moment.

Let's Create Something Beautiful

We'd love to hear about your vision and explore how we can bring it to life.
