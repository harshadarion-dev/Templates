We recommend booking 12-18 months in advance for weddings, especially during peak season (May-October). For corporate events and other celebrations, 3-6 months is typically sufficient. However, we do accommodate last-minute requests when possible, so don't hesitate to reach out regardless of your timeline.

Our packages range from day-of coordination (Silver) to full-service planning (Luxury). All packages include an initial consultation, vendor recommendations, timeline creation, and day-of management. Higher-tier packages include design services, full vendor management, budget tracking, and custom installations. Visit our Packages page for detailed breakdowns.
