Our Story

Founded in 2011, Real Estate Construction began with a simple mission: to make high-quality, architecturally significant homes accessible. We saw a gap in the market for turnkey properties that didn't compromise on materials or design.

Today, our team of over 100 dedicated architects, engineers, and builders work seamlessly to deliver projects from initial land surveying to the final coat of paint. We specialize in aerated concrete, a material we chose for its superior thermal insulation, longevity, and environmental benefits.

We don't just build houses; we build the backdrop for your family's memories.
