Nature's Edge

Sustainable Luxury | Completed 2023

Concept & Philosophy

Nature's Edge is a testament to sustainable living without the compromise of luxury. Anchored into a rugged, sloping topography, the architectural footprint was intentionally minimized to preserve the surrounding old-growth forest and natural drainage patterns.

The material palette relies heavily on locally sourced stone, rammed earth, and sustainable timber, allowing the structure to visually recede into the landscape as it weathers over time. A sweeping living roof not only acts as superior natural insulation but reinstates the micro-habitat displaced by the building's footprint.

Passive solar design principles were employed throughout. Deep overhangs protect the interior from harsh summer sun while capturing low winter light, resulting in a home that remains thermally comfortable year-round with minimal mechanical intervention.
