The Glass House

Urban Icon | Available Q3

Concept & Philosophy

The Glass House challenges the conventional boundaries of residential architecture by completely redefining transparency. Nestled within an urban periphery, the structure acts as a quiet pavilion that invites the surrounding environment into its core living spaces.

Engineered with a minimalist steel exoskeleton and floor-to-ceiling insulated glazing, the home receives an abundance of natural light throughout the day. The structural framing is kept deliberately slender, ensuring the architecture acts merely as a lens through which the changing seasons are observed.

Internally, the floor plan is completely open and free-flowing. Private quarters are concealed within a central, opaque core adorned with rich walnut paneling, providing a striking contrast to the luminous, transparent outer shell.
