Villa Sereno

Coastal Retreat | Completed 2024

Concept & Philosophy

Villa Sereno is an exploration of the boundary between the natural environment and built form. Situated along the serene coastline, the primary objective was to frame the expansive ocean views while maintaining a profound sense of interior intimacy and privacy.

We utilized a highly restrained palette of board-formed concrete, warm local timbers, and expansive glass. The architectural volume is broken down into staggered pavilions, reducing the overall mass of the building and allowing natural cross-ventilation to flow seamlessly through the living spaces.

The transition from indoors to outdoors is completely fluid. Retractable glass walls dissolve the living room into the outdoor terrace, prioritizing a lifestyle deeply connected to the coastal rhythms.
