Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to digital inclusion for all users.

Last Updated: April 28, 2026

LUPIXERY is deeply committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We continually improve the user experience for everyone and actively strive to apply the relevant accessibility standards to our website and digital communications.

1. Conformance Status

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) define requirements for designers and developers to improve accessibility for people with disabilities. It defines three levels of conformance: Level A, Level AA, and Level AAA.

LUPIXERY is partially conformant with WCAG 2.1 level AA. Partially conformant means that some parts of the content do not fully conform to the accessibility standard, though we are actively working to resolve these issues.

2. Accessibility Features

We have implemented the following features to ensure a highly accessible environment for our visitors:

  • Keyboard Navigation: Our website is designed to be fully navigable using a keyboard, allowing users who cannot use a mouse to easily access all content.
  • Alt Text for Images: We provide descriptive alternative text for all meaningful images, architectural renders, and photographs to assist users who rely on screen readers.
  • High Contrast: We carefully select typography and color palettes (such as our dark charcoal and gold) to maintain high contrast ratios, making text easier to read for visually impaired users.
  • Scalable Text: The website layout accommodates zooming up to 200% without loss of content or functionality.

3. Ongoing Efforts

Accessibility is not a one-time project, but an ongoing commitment. As our website evolves to feature new architectural portfolios and design insights, we regularly audit our site using automated tools and manual testing to ensure compliance and a barrier-free experience.

4. Feedback and Contact

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of LUPIXERY. If you encounter any accessibility barriers while browsing our site or need information in an alternative format, please let us know immediately so we can assist you.
