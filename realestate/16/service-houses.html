Timber Frame Houses

Modern and eco-friendly housing solutions tailored for you

Why choose timber frame?

Timber frame construction is known for its speed, energy efficiency, and flexibility in design. We use advanced Canadian technology to ensure your home is durable and comfortable in any climate. Our standard construction period starts from just 45 days, allowing you to move in faster compared to traditional building methods.

Every home comes with a comprehensive 7-year warranty. You can choose from our ready-made solutions ranging from 60 to 200 m², or opt for a completely individual design tailored to your specific needs and lifestyle.

Key Features & Benefits

  • Energy Efficiency: Class A rating with double frames and 200 mm premium insulation to retain heat in winter and keep cool in summer.
  • Ventilated Facade: Ensures breathability and prevents moisture buildup, extending the life of the structure.
  • Custom Foundations: Foundation tailored to your land: pile, strip, or slab depending on the soil and house design.
  • Zero Shrinkage: Unlike traditional log homes, our timber frames do not shrink, allowing for immediate interior finishing and move-in.
