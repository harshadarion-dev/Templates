Timber Frame Saunas

Eco-friendly, compact, and functional relaxation spaces

Perfect relaxation at home

Our timber frame saunas offer the ultimate relaxation experience right in your backyard. We build compact and highly functional saunas using only the best eco-friendly materials. With ready projects starting from 15 m², you can have your sauna up and running in no time.

We pride ourselves on providing high-quality interior finishing, utilizing premium aspen or linden wood which not only looks beautiful but also withstands extreme heat and moisture perfectly.

What's Included

  • Vapor and Waterproofing: Professional grade isolation to keep the heat inside and protect the timber frame.
  • Natural Wood Finish: High-quality natural lining throughout the interior for an authentic sauna feel.
  • Heat-resistant Glass: Durable and safe glass doors and windows that can handle high temperatures without issue.
  • Complete Stove Installation: We handle the full installation of your chosen sauna stove and chimney, ensuring perfect functionality and safety.
