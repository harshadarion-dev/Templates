About Us

Creating reliable homes for happy families for over 10 years

We build the future

The BuildHouse company was founded in 2013 with the aim to offer the market modern, eco-friendly, and affordable housing solutions. Over the years, we have built more than 180 properties across Russia.

Our specialization is timber frame houses and saunas built using Canadian and Scandinavian technologies. We use only certified materials and give a guarantee of up to 7 years on all structural elements.
