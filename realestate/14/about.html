Our Story

Excellence in
Every Detail

Founded in 2010, Luxury Estate has grown from a small boutique agency to a global leader in premium real estate. We have successfully closed over 140 luxury property transactions, earning 66 industry awards along the way.

Our approach combines deep market expertise with personalized service. We understand that buying or selling a luxury property is not just a transaction - it's a life-changing decision. That's why we dedicate ourselves to understanding your unique needs and aspirations.

With offices in Dubai, London, New York, and Singapore, we offer truly global reach with local expertise. Our network spans 35 countries, giving our clients access to the world's most exclusive properties.
