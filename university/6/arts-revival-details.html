A Revival of Renaissance Oil Painting Methods

A hands-on look at how classic arts are surviving and thriving in modern curriculums. Why are students abandoning iPad Procreate in favor of linseed oil and layered glazing?

Our Arts Faculty discusses the tactile sensation of physical creation and how limiting oneself to era-accurate tools opens up creative constraints that result in phenomenal mastery of lighting.
