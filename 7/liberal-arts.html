Liberal Arts

Providing a strong foundation in critical thinking, writing, history, philosophy, and sociology.

Program Overview

The College of Liberal Arts offers rigorous interdisciplinary programs designed to develop independent thinkers and global citizens. Our curriculum spans the humanities, social sciences, and expressive arts.

Core Areas of Study

  • History and Sociology
  • Philosophy and Ethics
  • Literature, Creative Writing, and Media
  • Political Science and International Relations

Career Opportunities

Our Liberal Arts graduates thrive as authors, educators, journalists, public servants, and diplomats who critically shape the ongoing dialogue of human society.
