Law & Public Policy

Renowned programs designed to train tomorrow's advocates, judges, and foundational policy makers.

Program Overview

The intersection of Law and Public Policy determines how our societies function. We equip future leaders with a deep understanding of jurisprudence, legislation, human rights, and policy formation.

Core Areas of Study

  • Constitutional and International Law
  • Public Administration and Policy Analysis
  • Human Rights and Environmental Advocacy
  • Corporate Law and Regulation

Career Opportunities

You will be primed for success as an attorney, lawmaker, policy advisor, or advocacy director in both government and top private firms worldwide.
